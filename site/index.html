31 · Aerodynamics

How do airplanes fly?

Scenario A plane accelerates, rotates, climbs, and can glide even when thrust is reduced.

The physics A moving wing redirects airflow. Lift opposes weight; thrust counters drag. Increasing angle of attack helps only up to a point: excessive angle can stall the wing.
